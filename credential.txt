SCHOOL ERP SYSTEM - DEMO LOGIN CREDENTIALS
===========================================

All demo accounts are created automatically when the database is seeded
(`php artisan migrate --seed`). The seeded admin password can be overridden
with the ADMIN_EMAIL / ADMIN_PASSWORD environment variables.

--------------------------------------------------------------------
ROLE             | EMAIL                 | PASSWORD
--------------------------------------------------------------------
Super Admin      | admin@school.com      | admin1234
Teacher          | teacher@school.com    | teacher1234
Staff            | staff@school.com      | staff1234
--------------------------------------------------------------------

Notes
-----
- Super Admin has full access to every module (dashboard, students,
  teachers, classes, subjects, fees, salaries, expenses, events,
  gallery, promotions, users & permissions).
- Teacher can manage attendances and results.
- Staff has basic staff-level access.
- Change the default passwords after your first login in a live
  environment.
